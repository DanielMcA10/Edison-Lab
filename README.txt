Edison Lab - standalone
1) Deploy this folder as a static site (Vercel: drag the folder onto vercel.com/new, or run `vercel` inside it).
2) Open the URL on your phone. Chrome: menu > Install app. iPhone Safari: Share > Add to Home Screen.
3) First load downloads ~11 MB and caches it; after that it runs offline.
Your decks are stored on that device (Decks tab > Backup & restore). To move decks from the Claude version: Decks tab > Backup & restore > Show my backup, copy it, then paste it into Restore in the new app.
To update: redeploy; the app refreshes on the next online open.
